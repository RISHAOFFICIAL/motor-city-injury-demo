What We Treat

Common Auto Accident Injuries

Car accidents cause injuries that are not always obvious right away. Our team is trained to find and treat the hidden damage that can cause long-term pain if left untreated.

Whiplash

Neck strain from sudden back-and-forth movement. Causes neck pain, stiffness, headaches, and dizziness. One of the most common car accident injuries.

Back & Spinal Injuries

Herniated discs, spinal misalignment, and lower back pain from impact forces. Early treatment prevents chronic pain.

Neck Pain & Stiffness

Muscle strain, pinched nerves, and reduced range of motion in the neck and shoulders after a collision.

Headaches & Migraines

Post-accident headaches caused by neck tension, concussion, or spinal misalignment. We identify the root cause.

Soft Tissue Injuries

Sprains, strains, bruising, and muscle damage throughout the body. Proper rehabilitation prevents scar tissue and stiffness.

Joint & Extremity Pain

Shoulder, knee, wrist, and ankle injuries from bracing during impact or direct contact with the vehicle interior.

Step by Step

What to Do After a Car Accident

The steps you take in the first days after an accident can make a big difference in your recovery and your insurance claim. Here is what to do.

Seek Medical Attention

Get checked even if you feel fine. Whiplash, concussions, and soft tissue injuries often do not show symptoms for days. Early evaluation protects your health and your claim.

Document Everything

Take photos of the scene and your injuries. Get the police report number. Save contact info for witnesses. Keep every medical record and receipt.

Contact Your Insurance

Michigan no-fault (PIP) covers your medical care after an auto accident regardless of who was at fault. Report the accident promptly and keep your claim number.

Schedule Your Evaluation

Call us to book your first appointment. We will assess your injuries, order X-rays if needed, and create a personalized treatment plan for your recovery.

Follow Your Treatment Plan

Consistent care leads to faster, more complete recovery. Attend your appointments, do your home exercises, and communicate with your care team.
